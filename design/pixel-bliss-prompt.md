The user-supplied pixel-art Bliss image was a visual reference for the scene, palette, and blocky style. This is a newly generated landscape, with no lettering or interface baked into it.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-bleed background for a retro hackathon website, wide landscape 1536 by 1024.
Primary request: An original pixel-art rendition inspired by the famous Bliss landscape and the user's reference: luminous rolling grassy green hills beneath a saturated blue sky with large drifting white clouds. It should instantly evoke that serene early-2000s desktop wallpaper feeling.
Composition: sky occupies the upper 58 percent, the crest of one broad hill rises gently on the left and slopes toward the right, then lush green grass fills the lower portion. Horizon is soft in shape but made entirely of stepped square pixels. Keep the central area simple enough for opaque retro application windows to be displayed over it. Scenic image fills every edge.
Style: visibly chunky low-resolution pixel art, coherent square pixel grid, approximately 256 by 170 logical pixels enlarged with nearest-neighbor edges, limited palette, broad flat clusters, occasional restrained dithering in the grass and cloud shadows. Similar block size and simplicity to the user's reference, no smooth gradients or anti-aliased curves.
Palette: vivid medium and light blue sky, white and pale blue clouds, lime and olive green hill highlights with deep green grass shadows.
Constraints: landscape only, no text, no lettering, no logo, no interface, no border, no people, no buildings, no trees, no sun disk, no watermark. Do not include a Windows logo.
